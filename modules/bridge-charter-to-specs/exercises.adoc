== Map a foreign charter

kind: drill

Take an example charter and write, for every field, which file in the repository
would carry it.

.Solution
[%collapsible]
====
The fields that regularly cause discussion are "Aufwandsschaetzung" and
"Rahmenbedingungen": neither has an openspec artefact, and both stay in the
charter. Not every field needs a counterpart — the mapping exists where a field
has a living counterpart, not for symmetry.
====

== Derive two capabilities from two objectives

kind: drill

Take two objectives from the example charter and write one capability spec for
each, with at least one requirement and scenario.

.Solution
[%collapsible]
====
Two checks on your result: does the scenario mention anything about the
implementation (it should not), and could a person outside the team decide
whether it holds (they should be able to). If either fails, the objective was
still a wish.
====

== Wire your milestones to changes

kind: project

For each milestone in your charter, name the change that must be archived and by
when, and record it in the milestone plan.
