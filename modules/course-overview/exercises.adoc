== Check your machine against the requirements

kind: drill

Go through the hardware checklist in the module `learning-environment-setup` and
write down, for your own machine: amount of RAM, free disk space, whether
virtualisation is enabled in the BIOS, and whether you have the BitLocker
recovery key.

.Solution
[%collapsible]
====
There is no single right answer — the point is the list. Any missing item is
your homework before the next lesson. The two that cost the most time later are
the recovery key and free space below 100 GB.
====

== Read one module's questions first

kind: drill

Open `questions.adoc` of a module you have not read yet and try to answer the
questions from what you already know. Then read the module and answer again.

.Solution
[%collapsible]
====
The second pass is faster and more precise. That is what the published questions
are for: they turn reading into looking for something.
====
