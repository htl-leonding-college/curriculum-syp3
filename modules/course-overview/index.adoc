== What this subject is about

The course has four areas. They are not four unrelated topics — they are the
outer frame, the inner work, the way you describe a system, and the tools you
need to do any of it.

[plantuml,course-areas,svg]
----
@startmindmap
skinparam monochrome true
skinparam shadowing false
* SYP year 3
** Governance
*** the outside: proposal, charter, milestones, acceptance
*** mandatory in every project form up to the diploma thesis
** Process models and SDD
*** the inside: how you get from goal to result
*** waterfall, Scrum and spec-driven development as three tempos
** Modelling
*** UML: use case, class, activity, state
*** diagrams as source text, not as pictures
** Tools
*** git, AsciiDoc, GitHub Actions and Pages
*** AI agents, Docker, compose, minikube
@endmindmap
----

The distinction that runs through the whole year:

[cols="1,2,2",options="header"]
|===
|  |Governance (outside) |Execution (inside)

|What it fixes
|goals, deliverable, milestones, responsibility
|how the work is done

|Who prescribes it
|the school, the client, later the diploma thesis rules
|your team

|Changes during the project?
|no — it is frozen and stays the yardstick
|constantly, and visibly

|Where it lives
|`docs/` — proposal, charter, milestone plan
|`openspec/specs/`, `openspec/changes/`, `src/`
|===

A project charter never says "use Scrum". It asks what you want to achieve and
by when. That is why the execution side could change — from Scrum to
spec-driven development — without touching the governance side at all.

== How the year runs

A lesson is three units: one unit of theory, two units of practice.

----
1 UE theory     the idea, the vocabulary, the decision
2 UE practice   your hands on the keyboard
----

The order is not the order of a textbook. It follows what you need to be able to
do next: git before you can hand anything in, AsciiDoc before documentation
means anything, an agent before spec-driven development is more than a claim.

Project work happens *outside* the lesson. Lesson time is input, review and
getting unstuck. Three lessons in the year are milestone reviews, where the team
shows what exists and compares estimate against reality.

== Assessment

You are assessed in an *oral examination*. That is a deliberate choice and it
follows from something you already know: an agent produces a plausible solution
to almost any exercise in seconds. An exercise handed in is therefore no longer
proof of anything.

So the exercises stop pretending to be proof and become what they are — an
occasion to learn. Every module ships its exam questions with it, in
`questions.adoc`. Nothing is hidden, nothing is unlocked later.

[TIP]
====
Read the questions of a module *before* you work through it. They tell you what
the module is for, which is more useful than reading the text twice.
====

== Decisions

* The course language is English. Governance terms are additionally taught in
  German, because the forms you fill in later — the diploma thesis application
  above all — are German.
* Everyone works on Ubuntu 26.04 LTS or macOS. WSL2 is not an option here; the
  module on the learning environment says why.
* Solutions are not hidden. The proof of competence is the oral exam.
* Exercises marked `project` refer to your own team project and have no model
  solution.

== Pitfalls

* Treating the exercise as the deliverable. The deliverable is that you can
  explain it afterwards.
* Postponing the environment setup. Everything from the second lesson on assumes
  a working machine, and a broken installation costs an afternoon, not ten
  minutes.
* Reading only the theory unit. Half the subject is in your hands, not in the
  slides.
