== Run one 6-3-5 round on a given topic

kind: drill

Topic: "something at this school that wastes people's time every week". Six
people, three ideas, five minutes, pass on. Do at least three rounds.

.Solution
[%collapsible]
====
No model solution — but two observations are reliable. Round one produces the
obvious ideas everybody already had; rounds three and later produce the ones
worth discussing, because by then the obvious ones are used up and you are
forced to build on what is on the sheet. If a round produces nothing, it is
usually because people stopped reading before writing.
====

== Collect and cluster the sheets

kind: drill

Collect the sheets from the exercise above and group the ideas into clusters of
related ones. Count how many distinct clusters remain.

.Solution
[%collapsible]
====
Typically five to ten clusters from a hundred ideas. That reduction is the
hand-over point to the next module: clusters are candidates, and the weighted
scoring decides between them.
====

== Produce your team's candidate list

kind: project

From your own round, write down the three candidates your team would actually be
willing to work on for a year.
