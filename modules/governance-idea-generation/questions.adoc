== Why are generating and selecting ideas kept apart?

covers: lo-1

Expected answer points:

* Opposite rules: quantity without evaluation versus evaluation against criteria
* Evaluating early makes the group defend ideas instead of producing them
* Separation is in time, not only in intention

== Describe the 6-3-5 procedure and say what it produces

covers: lo-2

Expected answer points:

* Six people, three ideas, five minutes, sheet passes on, six rounds
* 108 written ideas, of which a handful are worth pursuing
* Written output, everyone contributes, time-boxed by construction, forced
  building on others

== Name two other techniques and the situation each suits

covers: lo-3

Expected answer points:

* Mind mapping — one person or a pair, unclear structure, visual
* SCAMPER — a stuck group with an existing solution, forces variation
* Neither is practised in this course; 6-3-5 is

== Why is "ask an agent for fifty ideas" not a replacement?

covers: lo-1, lo-3

Expected answer points:

* Produces plausible averages, not ideas the team cares about
* Commitment matters for a project that has to survive the whole year
* The technique also produces the written raw material for the proposals
