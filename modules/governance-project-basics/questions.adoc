== Which criteria make a piece of work a project? Give an example that fails one

covers: lo-1

Expected answer points:

* Unique, time-bounded, resourced, goal-directed, complex enough to organise
* Counter-example: routine work such as support mail — continuous, repeating,
  no unique result

== What belongs in an initial situation, and what does not?

covers: lo-2

Expected answer points:

* Who has the problem, what happens today, what it costs
* Not the solution, not the technology
* Test: a reader who does not know your solution must still recognise the problem

== Name the three constraints and explain which one moves in a school year

covers: lo-3

Expected answer points:

* Scope, time, resources
* Time and resources are fixed by the school year and the team
* Scope is the only variable; if it is treated as fixed too, quality gives way
  silently

== Where does the initial situation live in the project repository, and why there?

covers: lo-2

Expected answer points:

* `openspec/config.yaml` under `context:`
* One text instead of two; the agent and the supervisor read the same thing
