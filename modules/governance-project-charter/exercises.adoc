== Sort ten sentences into the right fields

kind: drill

Take ten sentences from an example application, shuffled, and assign each to the
field it belongs in.

.Solution
[%collapsible]
====
The two that regularly land in the wrong field: a sentence naming the technology
belongs in the technology section, not in the initial situation; and "we want to
learn Spring Boot" is neither an objective nor a research objective — it is a
personal motivation and belongs nowhere in the form.
====

== Turn three activities into milestones

kind: drill

Rewrite as milestones: "build the database", "do the frontend", "write tests".

.Solution
[%collapsible]
====
For example: "Members and sessions can be stored and read back; the schema is
documented — 15 Nov"; "A trainer can record attendance for one session in the
browser — 10 Dec"; "The booking rules are covered by tests that run in CI — 20
Dec". Each names a checkable result and a date.
====

== Two variants and a decision

kind: drill

A climbing gym wants its visitors to book time slots online instead of phoning
the front desk. Describe three variants on one page, compare them on four
criteria of your choice, and write the variant decision in two sentences.

.Solution
[%collapsible]
====
For example: a hosted room-booking service, a web application of your own,
and a shared calendar with a booking form in front of it. Criteria could be
cost per year, effort to build, how well the gym's own rules can be enforced
(at most 40 visitors per slot, members book seven days ahead, day guests three), and who
operates it afterwards.

The decision names the variant *and the reason*: "A web application of our own,
because only it can enforce the gym's booking rules; the hosted service would be
cheaper to run, but the owner rejected its fixed rules." A decision without a
reason is a preference.
====
