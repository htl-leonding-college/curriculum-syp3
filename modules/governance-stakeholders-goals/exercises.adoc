== Find the missing stakeholder

kind: drill

A team builds a system that stores which student borrowed which piece of lab
equipment. They list: students, the lab supervisor, themselves. Who is missing,
and what can that person do to the project?

.Solution
[%collapsible]
====
At least: whoever operates the server afterwards (can refuse to host it), the
school's data protection contact (personal data plus borrowing history), and the
teacher who accepts the project. Any of them can stop the project at the end,
which is the most expensive moment to be stopped.
====

== Rewrite five wishes as goals

kind: drill

Rewrite: "easy to use", "secure", "modern", "reliable", "works on phones".

.Solution
[%collapsible]
====
Each needs an observable action and a condition, for example "a first-time user
completes a booking without instructions in under 90 seconds" or "the site is
usable at a viewport width of 360 px without horizontal scrolling". If you
cannot state how it would be checked, it stays a wish — and it belongs in the
proposal as one, not in the goals table.
====

== Write the goals and non-goals for your project

kind: project

Fill in the goals table and the non-goals section of your charter — at least
three goals, each with the evidence that verifies it, and at least three
non-goals.
