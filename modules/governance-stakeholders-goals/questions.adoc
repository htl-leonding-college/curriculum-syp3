== Who counts as a stakeholder, and which four types should you look for?

covers: lo-1

Expected answer points:

* Anyone affected by the project or able to affect it
* Users, client, operators, affected parties (for example data subjects)
* The dangerous quadrant is high influence with low interest

== Turn "the app should be fast" into a verifiable goal

covers: lo-2

Expected answer points:

* Observable action, measurable condition, context
* For example: attendance list for 30 members opens in under one second on the
  lab laptops
* A third party must be able to check it without asking what you meant

== Why does a charter contain non-goals?

covers: lo-3

Expected answer points:

* They prevent scope creep during the project and disappointment at acceptance
* One line each, cheap
* A non-goal is not a permanent refusal, it is out of scope for this project

== Why is "we implement a REST API" a bad goal?

covers: lo-2, lo-3

Expected answer points:

* It measures effort, not result
* Nobody outside the team cares how it is built
* Rewrite it as what a user can then do

== What is the risk in the high-influence, low-interest quadrant?

covers: lo-1

Expected answer points:

* They are not watching until they say no
* Keep them satisfied and informed early, when a veto is still cheap
