== Score three laptops

kind: drill

Build a weighted scoring for buying a laptop for school use, with at least five
criteria and three alternatives of your choice.

.Solution
[%collapsible]
====
Any table works if the criteria discriminate. A common mistake is a criterion
like "has a keyboard": every alternative scores 10 and the criterion contributes
nothing but its weight to every column equally. Compare your criteria against
that test before you rate anything.
====

== Break your own table

kind: drill

Take the table from the previous exercise and change weights until the loser
wins. Note how much you had to change.

.Solution
[%collapsible]
====
If a small change flips the result, the alternatives are genuinely close and the
table should not be presented as if it decided anything. If you had to move
twenty or thirty points of weight, the result is robust — which is exactly the
information you want before defending the decision.
====

== Choose your team's project

kind: project

Score your three candidate ideas with criteria and weights your team agrees on,
fix the weights first, and put the table into your project proposal.
