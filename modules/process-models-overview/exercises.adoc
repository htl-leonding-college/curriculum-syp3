== Map three projects onto the pattern

kind: drill

For each — a school festival, a payroll system, a prototype for a hackathon —
write down what "clarify the goal", "verify" and "control the change" concretely
mean, and which tempo fits.

.Solution
[%collapsible]
====
The interesting one is the payroll system: verification is external (an audit),
being wrong is expensive and visible, so the loop stays long even though the
technology would allow a short one. Tempo follows the cost of error, not the
tooling.
====

== Find the loop in a model you were not taught here

kind: drill

Look up Kanban or the spiral model and identify the six steps of the invariant
pattern in it.

.Solution
[%collapsible]
====
They are always there. Kanban's "definition of done" is the verification step,
its WIP limit bounds the scope, and the pull signal controls when a change is
taken in. Being able to find the pattern in an unfamiliar model is the point of
learning it as a pattern.
====
