== Turn three prose sentences into requirements with scenarios

kind: drill

Rewrite: "the system should be secure", "users can manage their bookings", "the
export must work".

.Solution
[%collapsible]
====
Each needs one observable statement per behaviour, for example: "The system SHALL
refuse a booking change less than two hours before the session start" with a
scenario stating when and what the member sees. "Secure" is not one requirement
but several — authentication, authorisation, data retention — and splitting it is
the actual work.
====

== Find the drift

kind: drill

Take a requirements document written for an earlier project (your own or a
partner's) and mark every sentence that no longer describes the system.

.Solution
[%collapsible]
====
Typically a third of it. The relevant observation is that nothing announced the
drift — no build failed, no test broke. That is the property specs plus a check
are meant to restore.
====

== Write the functional specification for your project

kind: project

Write the functional specification for your project from
`templates/governance/pflichtenheft.adoc`, once, right after the charter is
approved. Fill chapters 1 to 5 and 7, number every requirement in chapter 4, and
trace each one back to a goal in chapter 3. Go through it with your client and
note the date of that agreement in the document.

== Write the first capability spec for your project

kind: project

Take the functional requirements of one area from chapter 4 of your functional
specification and write them as one `openspec/specs/<capability>/spec.md` with at
least two requirements, each with a scenario. Keep the requirement numbers
visible.
