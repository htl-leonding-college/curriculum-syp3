* Every topic has *rules*, not just data entry — something to specify and test
* Every topic has *several roles* with different interests
* Every topic has its own *situation at the screen* — a JavaFX desktop
  application, no mobile app
* Start small: a core for milestone 1, extensions for the later milestones

[.notes]
--
Choose a topic in which somebody in your team has a contact person. That person
becomes the client and sits on the steering board.

== Volunteer fire brigade (Freiwillige Feuerwehr)

The commander keeps members, courses and equipment checks in three spreadsheets
— and nobody knows who may wear breathing apparatus this month.

* *Rules:* qualifications expire (medical check, yearly exercise); equipment has
  inspection due dates; only qualified members count for a crew
* *Roles:* commander, training officer, equipment keeper, members
* *At the screen:* office PC in the fire station; a status board in the vehicle
  hall — readable from three metres

[.notes]
--
Milestone 1: members and qualifications with expiry dates. Extensions:
exercise planning and attendance, equipment inspections, yearly report for the
district command.
--

== Brass band (Musikkapelle)

The archive holds 800 pieces in folders; for every performance the bandmaster
asks around who still has which part.

* *Rules:* a performance needs a minimum number of players per register; a part
  or a loan instrument is with exactly one person; returns are due after the
  season
* *Roles:* chairperson, bandmaster, archivist, instrument keeper, musicians
* *At the screen:* the archivist searching and lending at the desk; the
  bandmaster putting together a programme by drag and drop

[.notes]
--
Milestone 1: sheet music archive with search and lending. Extensions:
performances with line-up check, loan instruments, attendance at rehearsals.
--

== Restaurant

Orders are written on paper, carried to the kitchen and typed into the till at
the end — and the bill for a table of eight is split by hand.

* *Rules:* tables have capacities and time slots; an order goes through states
  (ordered, cooking, served, paid); a bill can be split by person; allergens are
  shown
* *Roles:* guest, waiting staff, kitchen, owner
* *At the screen:* a touch till for the waiting staff — fast, big targets; a
  kitchen display read from two metres while cooking

[.notes]
--
Milestone 1: menu, order per table, bill. Extensions: kitchen display,
reservations, split bills, daily sales report.
--

== Cinema (Kino)

A small cinema with three halls plans its programme on a whiteboard and sells
tickets from a printed seat plan.

* *Rules:* a show needs a free hall for film, adverts and cleaning time; no seat
  is sold twice; price levels (child, student, cinema day)
* *Roles:* customer, cashier, programme planner, manager
* *At the screen:* the box office clicking seats in the seat plan while the
  queue waits; a self-service terminal in the foyer that needs no explanation

[.notes]
--
Milestone 1: programme planning with conflict check. Extensions: seat booking,
price levels, self-service terminal, occupancy statistics.
--

== Driving school (Fahrschule)

The office books lessons in a paper diary and checks by phone whether instructor
and car are free.

* *Rules:* a lesson needs instructor, vehicle and learner at the same time;
  mandatory lessons per licence class; exam only when the prerequisites are met
* *Roles:* office, instructors, learners, management
* *At the screen:* reception with the customer at the counter — keyboard,
  shortcuts, conflicts shown at once; the instructor entering progress after the
  lesson

[.notes]
--
Milestone 1: booking lessons with conflict check. Extensions: training
progress per learner, exam registration, printout "what is still missing".
--

== Winter road service (Straßenmeisterei)

The depot manager plans winter duty and on-call weeks in a spreadsheet and
checks rest periods from memory.

* *Rules:* rest periods after night shifts; only qualified drivers for each
  vehicle; every route covered; shift swaps must keep all rules
* *Roles:* depot manager, dispatcher, drivers
* *At the screen:* the dispatcher at a large screen with roster and routes; a
  touch terminal in the vehicle hall where drivers report with gloves on

[.notes]
--
Milestone 1: duty roster with qualification check. Extensions: rest period
check, shift swaps, report of routes and spreading material. Needs a contact
person at a road depot — otherwise the team cannot elicit requirements.
--
